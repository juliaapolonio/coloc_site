Overview

Overlap = loci where at least one gene nominated here was also reported in the literature (concordant). “This study” alone = loci reported in the literature, but with no gene in common (discordant). “Literature” alone = loci with a previous report that did not reach significance here. The isolated bubble holds loci with no literature counterpart at all.

NoteHow concordance is calculated

Concordant = at least one gene in common between the genes that colocalized on this analysis (genes_significant) and the union of literature sources (BigBrain eQTL/sQTL, Bellenguez, SingleBrain). Discordant = there’s literature for the locus, but none of the genes coincides. Literature only = the locus was reported before, but nothing reached significance here. No comparison = none of the sources covers this locus (includes all 19 new loci from consensus meta-analysis).
