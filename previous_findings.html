Overview

Overlap = loci where at least one gene nominated here was also reported before (concordant). “This study” alone = both sources report genes, but none in common (discordant). “Literature” alone = previously reported, nothing significant here. The isolated bubble holds loci colocalized here with no literature counterpart. Out of 97 tested loci, 9 colocalized nowhere and have no previous report, so they fall outside the diagram.

NoteHow concordance is calculated

The denominator is the set of loci tested in this analysis, taken from coloc_all_annotated.csv. Genes nominated here are those with PP.H4 ≥ 0.8; literature genes are the union of the available sources (BigBrain eQTL/sQTL, Bellenguez, SingleBrain), matched to each locus both by name and by distance between GWAS lead variants. Concordant = at least one gene in common. Discordant = both sources report genes for the locus, none in common. Literature only = previously reported, nothing reached significance here. New (no literature) = colocalized here, no previous report. Loci that colocalized nowhere and have no previous report are counted in the total but fall outside the diagram.
